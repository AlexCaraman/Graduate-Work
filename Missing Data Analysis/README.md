- Final Exam: multiple imputation steps and questions, fully conditional versus predictive mean matching. Imputation analysis with marital status data, survey versus non-survey procedures
- HW1: MNAR, MAR, MCAR with t-tests and visualizations. Complete-case analysis, and regression/mean imputation, and propensity score (non-response weighting) method on air quality data

- HW3: MICE library for imputation. Rubin’s methods for combining imputed results (between and within imputation variance). Comparisons to complete case analysis, predictive mean matching, multivariate normal and FCS imputation on mammal sleep data. 
- Midterm Exam: Predicting MNAR vs MAR vs MCAR, effect of certain imputation methods on estimated strength of relationships. Comparing propensity (non-response weighting) method with t-tests on dgestat data. EM algorithm, regression imputation.
